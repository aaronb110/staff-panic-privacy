Staff Panic! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how Staff Panic! handles information when you use the game.

Information we collect

The current version of Staff Panic! does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of Staff Panic! does not include an advertising network or analytics service.

Game progress

Staff Panic! may store game information and preferences locally on your device so that the game can remember your progress and settings. This information is not sent to us by the current version of the game.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share personal information collected through Staff Panic!, as the current version of the game does not directly collect personal information.

Children's privacy

Staff Panic! does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current version of the game.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy if Staff Panic! changes, including if we introduce features such as advertising, analytics, online accounts or other online services. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have a question about privacy or Staff Panic!, contact us at staff.panic.game@gmail.com.
