Staff Panic! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Staff Panic! is a mobile game made by Staff Panic! Games, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and what choices you have.

The short version

We don't run our own servers. The game uses Google services to show ads, save your progress, handle purchases, measure how the game is played and send optional notifications. Those services collect some data, as explained below. We never sell your data.

1. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads. Rewarded ads are optional and give you a reward, such as free coins. Interstitial ads are full-screen ads shown between shifts. AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as phone model, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Google uses this to show ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. If you agree, it's also used to show ads based on your interests.

Your choices:

  • If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, the game asks for your consent when you first open it. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings in the game's settings menu.
  • If you're in a US state with privacy laws, you can opt out of your data being used for targeted advertising through the same Privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Privacy, then Ads.
  • You can buy "Remove Ads" to permanently remove interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay available if you want them.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2. Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are too hard, so we can improve it. It only starts after the game has checked your consent choices, using the same consent message as for ads.

  • If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, it only runs if you allow "Store and/or access information on a device" in that message.
  • It's switched off for anyone Google Play tells us is under 13.

What it collects:

  • gameplay events: starting, winning or failing a level (with the level, the venue, your stars and why a shift failed), finishing the tutorial, and watching a reward video
  • standard app events: first opening the game, sessions and how long they last, app updates and in-app purchases
  • a random ID for your copy of the game (an "app instance ID"), which changes if you reinstall
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, app version and language
  • your approximate region (country or city), worked out from your IP address. Google doesn't keep the IP address itself.

What it doesn't collect: your name, email address, advertising ID, a user account ID or your precise location. We don't use it for advertising.

Google processes this data for us as a service provider. If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, you can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings in the game's settings menu. Anyone can ask us to stop or delete analytics data by emailing us.

3. Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

When you finish a venue, the game offers to tell you when the next one opens. This is optional, and nothing is sent unless you say yes.

  • Android asks your permission before the game can show notifications.
  • To deliver them, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your copy of the game a device token (a random code that lets Google reach your phone). It doesn't contain your name or contact details.
  • To turn notifications off, go to your phone's Settings, then Apps, Staff Panic!, Notifications. Uninstalling the game also stops them.

4. Sign-in, cloud saves and achievements (Google Play Games Services)

The game signs you in automatically with your Google Play Games profile, so it doesn't need a separate account or password. Your game progress, coins, staff and settings are saved to your Google Play Games account, so your progress survives if you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. Achievements you unlock are recorded there too.

This data is stored by Google, not by us. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Coin packs, special packs, Remove Ads and the Career Pass are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment. The game never sees your card or payment details. It only receives confirmation of what you bought so it can unlock the item.

6. Age check in the US (Google Play Age Signals)

On phones in the US, the game may ask Google Play whether you're a known minor, as some US state laws require. Your age stays on your phone. It's only used to show safer ads to known minors and to keep game analytics off for anyone under 13. It's never sent to us.

7. Feedback and ratings

"Send feedback" in the game opens your own email app with your message filled in, plus the game version, your level, your phone model, Android version and screen size to help us fix bugs. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. We use your email only to reply to you.

The game may occasionally show Google Play's rating card. Your rating goes to Google Play, not to us.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, contacts, photos, precise location, or camera or microphone access. We don't use crash reporting tools, and we don't sell your data.

Legal basis (UK and EU players)

We rely on your consent for personalised ads and for game analytics. We rely on legitimate interests for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention. Cloud saves, purchases and notifications you asked for rely on providing the service you requested.

How long data is kept

We don't hold data about you on our own systems. Data stored on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the game. Cloud saves stay in your Google Play Games account until you delete them in your Google Play Games settings. Detailed game analytics data is kept for 2 months, then deleted by Google (summary reports with no individual data can be kept longer). Google keeps ad and purchase data in line with its own privacy policy. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with them.

International transfers

Google may process data outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States, using the legal safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used. Most of the data described here is held by Google, so the quickest route is usually through your Google account settings. You're also welcome to contact us. If you're in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Staff Panic! is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If Google Play tells us a player is under 13, the game shows only child-safe ads and keeps game analytics switched off.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page.
